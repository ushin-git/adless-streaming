# Privacy

Last updated: 2026-10-29

Adless Streaming has no listener account, advertising, analytics, or in-app tracking. The app does not send the local music library, likes, playlists, listening history, or queue to Adless Streaming servers. Local library data and preferences are stored on the device. Android cloud backup and device-transfer backup are disabled for this app.

## Network requests

When you search, the query is sent to the music sources you have enabled. When you play a remote item, the selected provider receives requests needed to stream it. Those services may receive standard connection data such as your IP address and request metadata under their own privacy policies and terms. Jamendo requires a developer client ID; it is a public application identifier, not a listener credential. The app does not send local audio files to remote services.

## Data retention and controls

Searches are not stored by Adless Streaming. Likes, playlists, and listening history remain on-device and can be cleared by uninstalling the app or clearing its app data. Online providers may process request data according to their own policies; see the provider links in [the source review](source-review.md).

The app has no crash-reporting or analytics SDK. Android and device vendors may collect diagnostics according to the device settings and policies.

## Contact

For privacy questions, open an issue at [github.com/onmacgit/adless-streaming](https://github.com/onmacgit/adless-streaming/issues). Do not include private listening history or other personal data in a public issue.
